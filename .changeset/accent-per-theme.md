`--ow-accent` now has a value per theme, like the other semantic tokens: `#2563eb` on light and
`#2f6fe8` on dark, a blue that carries white `--ow-on-accent` text at 4.5:1 or more in both themes.
The single pale value it had before barely showed on the light background (about 1.5:1).
