Add Context Menu to `owsem-ui/base`: Base UI's `ContextMenu.Root`, `Trigger`, `Portal`,
`Positioner`, `Popup`, `Item`, `LinkItem`, `CheckboxItem`, `RadioItem`, the indicators, `Group`,
`GroupLabel`, `Separator`, `SubmenuRoot`, and `SubmenuTrigger`. It is Menu opened by a right click
or a long press at the pointer: the popup, items, Glide highlight, and classes are Menu's, and the
area is yours, with no look from the library. Both flavors, CSS and Tailwind.
