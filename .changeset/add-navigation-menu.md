Add Navigation Menu to `owsem-ui/base`: Base UI's `NavigationMenu.Root`, `List`, `Item`, `Trigger`,
`Icon`, `Content`, `Link`, `Portal`, `Positioner`, `Popup`, `Viewport`, `Arrow`, and `Backdrop`. The
triggers are ghost buttons in Button's 32, 40, and 48px sizes with a chevron they draw themselves; the
panels share one box that moves to the open trigger, resizes to its content, and slides the content in
from the side of travel; links in a panel are cards lit by Glide. An empty `Portal` draws the
`Positioner`, `Popup`, and `Viewport`. Both flavors, CSS and Tailwind.
