Add Number Field, from `owsem-ui/base`, with every part of Base UI's: `Root`, `Group`, `Input`,
`Decrement`, `Increment`, `ScrubArea`, and `ScrubAreaCursor`. With no children `Root` draws a `Group`
with a minus button, an input, and a plus button. The Group is the field, drawn as Input is (`size`
`sm`, `md`, `lg` on `Root`: 32, 40, 48 px) with flat square buttons inset 4px; the scrub cursor is a
two-way arrow with a halo. Both flavors, CSS and Tailwind.
