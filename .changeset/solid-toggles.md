Toggle and Toggle Group take `variant="solid"` (default `"soft"`, today's look): the on state, or the
thumb and the pressed keys, is filled like the primary Button. In a Toolbar, render a
`<Toggle variant="solid" />`. Both CSS and Tailwind flavors; `ToggleVariant` and `ToggleGroupVariant` are exported.
