Add a Tailwind CSS v4 flavor of every component: the same `<name>.tsx`, with the styles as utility
classes in `<name>-variants.ts`. Glide, the hover highlight several of them share, comes along in
`lib/glide/`. Copy them from the docs' Manual install. `owsem-ui/tailwind.css` also carries the
keyframes, variants, and registered properties they use.
