Add a Tailwind CSS v4 flavor of Button, Separator, Kbd, Toggle, Input, Field, Fieldset, and Form:
the same `<name>.tsx`, with the styles as utility classes in `<name>-variants.ts`. Copy them from
the docs' Manual install. `owsem-ui/tailwind.css` also carries the keyframes and variants they use.
