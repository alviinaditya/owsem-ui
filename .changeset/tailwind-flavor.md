Add a Tailwind CSS v4 flavor of Button, Separator, Kbd, Toggle, Input, Field, Fieldset, Form,
Switch, Checkbox, Icon Morph, Tooltip, and Copy Button: the same `<name>.tsx`, with the styles as
utility classes in `<name>-variants.ts`. Copy them from the docs' Manual install.
`owsem-ui/tailwind.css` also carries the keyframes, variants, and registered properties they use.
