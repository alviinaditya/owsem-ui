Add a Tailwind CSS v4 flavor of Button, Separator, Kbd, Toggle, Input, Field, Fieldset, Form,
Switch, Checkbox, Icon Morph, Tooltip, Copy Button, Popover, Dialog, Scroll Area, Table, Radio
Group, Toggle Group, and Tabs: the same `<name>.tsx`, with the styles as utility classes in
`<name>-variants.ts`. Glide, the hover highlight they share, comes along in `lib/glide/`. Copy them
from the docs' Manual install. `owsem-ui/tailwind.css` also carries the keyframes, variants, and
registered properties they use.
