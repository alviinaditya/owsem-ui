Toggle is a button with two states, on and off. Off, it is flat and tinted under the pointer; on,
it is a raised key with crisp edges. Its sizes and spacing are Button's. Scroll to start the tour;
the stage follows each part.

## A key that stays up [#pressing]

On is a material, not a color. An on toggle is the raised key: a flat fill with a 1px edge darker
than the fill and a light line inside it, like a selected segment. Off, it is flat like a ghost
button: muted text, and a faint tint under the pointer. Text turns from muted to full strength.

Every press goes in first. While held, the toggle shrinks to 0.97 and takes the pressed material
within 90 ms, then lets go on the settle spring, with a small overshoot, and comes up raised or
flat. Under reduced motion it changes material without moving.

The stage plays it: the pointer turns Italic and Underline on, then off again, and the note's text
follows them. Play scene runs it again; press the toggles yourself to feel the press.

## Button's size and spacing [#geometry]

Toggle is a one-line control, so it follows Button's geometry. `--py` is the only input: the block
padding is `max(--py, (24px - 1lh) / 2)`, the inline padding adds `(1lh - 1cap) / 2` so the text
looks as padded at the sides as at the top, and the height is always `2 × --py + 1lh`. Icons are
the font size plus 2px, pulled toward the edge so they sit as far from the side as from the top.
Without children, a toggle is a square.

The stage measures every size live: with an icon and a label, a label only, or an icon only, each
size has one height.

| Size | Font | `--py` | Height | Icon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 13px | `0.5em` | 29.25px | 15px |
| `md` | 14px | `0.75em` | 38.5px | 16px |
| `lg` | 16px | `0.875em` | 48px | 18px |

Set `--py` in `style` on the toggle itself to change its height. It never drops under 24px, the
smallest target WCAG 2.5.8 allows.

## Anatomy [#anatomy]

Two parts. Base UI renders a `<button>` with `aria-pressed`, `data-pressed` while on, and
`data-disabled`; OwsemUI adds the `ow-toggle` class and `data-size`, and wraps `startIcon` and
`endIcon` in a span each. Inspect (I) outlines them on the stage.

| Part | Element | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Part name="Root" /> | `button.ow-toggle` | `aria-pressed`, `data-pressed`, `data-size`, and `data-icon-only` without children |
| <Part name="Icon" /> | `span.ow-toggle-icon` | Decorative (`aria-hidden`), `data-side="start"` or `"end"`; an svg or img inside fills it |

## States and variants [#states]

The stage sets the light and dark themes side by side: off, on, and disabled both ways. The pointer
rests on the off toggle in each theme, then turns it on and off.

| State | What shows it |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Flat, with muted text |
| Hover, while off | The `--ow-hover` tint, and text at full strength |
| Pressing | Scaled to 0.97 within 90 ms, in the pressed material: `--ow-pressed-bg` and `--ow-pressed-shadow` |
| On | The raised key: `--ow-raised-bg` and `--ow-raised-shadow`, with text in `--ow-fg` |
| Hover, while on | The fill tints 4% toward the text; the edges stay |
| Keyboard focus | A 2px ring 2px out, in `--ow-focus` |
| Disabled | 45% opacity, and presses do nothing |

An on toggle keeps its edges under the pointer, so hovering never looks like turning it off.

## Keyboard and accessibility [#keyboard]

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab | Moves focus to the toggle |
| Space, Enter | Turns it on or off |

Screen readers announce a toggle button and its state through `aria-pressed`. Keep its name the
same in both states, such as "Bold": the state is what changes. A toggle without children needs an
`aria-label`; its icons are `aria-hidden`.

With `prefers-reduced-motion`, a press changes material without shrinking. In forced colors
(Windows High Contrast), the toggle keeps a 1px edge in the system color, an on toggle is filled
with `Highlight`, and the focus ring is `Highlight` too. Right to left, `startIcon` sits on the
right.

## Customization [#customization]

The stage controls are open: pick the on surface or its text, and the code follows.

<ForMode mode="theme">

Component tokens override the theme tokens for every Toggle inside the element that sets them,
such as `.note`.

| Component token | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ow-toggle-fg` | `--ow-muted` | Text while off |
| `--ow-toggle-hover-bg` | `--ow-hover` | Tint under the pointer while off |
| `--ow-toggle-hover-fg` | `--ow-fg` | Text under the pointer while off |
| `--ow-toggle-on-bg` | `--ow-raised-bg` | Fill while on |
| `--ow-toggle-on-shadow` | `--ow-raised-shadow` | Edges while on |
| `--ow-toggle-on-fg` | `--ow-fg` | Text while on or held down |
| `--ow-toggle-pressed-bg` | `--ow-pressed-bg` | Fill while held down |
| `--ow-toggle-pressed-shadow` | `--ow-pressed-shadow` | Edges while held down |

</ForMode>

<ForMode mode="isolated">

Each Toggle carries its own values on its root and reads no shared token, so set them on the
toggle itself, as `note.css` does with `.note .ow-toggle`.

| Component token | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ow-toggle-fg` | Its own, per theme | Text while off |
| `--ow-toggle-hover-bg` | Its own, per theme | Tint under the pointer while off |
| `--ow-toggle-hover-fg` | Its own, per theme | Text under the pointer while off |
| `--ow-toggle-on-bg` | Its own, per theme | Fill while on |
| `--ow-toggle-on-shadow` | Its own, per theme | Edges while on |
| `--ow-toggle-on-fg` | Its own, per theme | Text while on or held down |
| `--ow-toggle-pressed-bg` | Its own, per theme | Fill while held down |
| `--ow-toggle-pressed-shadow` | Its own, per theme | Edges while held down |

</ForMode>

The corners follow `--ow-radius`, and the focus ring `--ow-focus`.
