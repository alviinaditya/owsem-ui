Tooltip names a button for people with a pointer or a keyboard: a short label, and its shortcut, on
a solid dark surface. The first one waits a moment; the next ones open at once. Scroll to start the
tour; the stage follows each part.

## One delay per group [#group]

A tooltip that opened at every pass of the pointer would flicker across a toolbar, so the first one
waits: 600 ms by default. Once it is open, the wait is over for the whole group: moving along the
toolbar opens each neighbor at once, with no animation. Stay off the toolbar for more than 400 ms,
and the next tooltip waits again.

The group is a `Tooltip.Provider` around the triggers, with one `delay` for all of them. Without a
Provider, each trigger waits on its own.

The first tooltip grows out of its trigger in 125 ms, from 0.97 and transparent to full size,
eased out, and leaves in 100 ms. Neighbors that open at once skip the animation, and so do
tooltips opened by keyboard focus.

The stage plays it: the pointer rests on Bold until its tooltip appears, slides along the toolbar,
steps off, and comes back once the wait has reset. Play scene runs it again; set Delay to Each in
the controls to feel the difference.

## Placed by its trigger [#placement]

Base UI places the tooltip on the `side` you ask for, `top` by default, 8px from the trigger, and
flips it to the other side when it would leave the screen. The arrow follows: it points at the
trigger's middle even when the tooltip slides along to stay on screen, and the tooltip grows from
the arrow's tip.

`inline-start` and `inline-end` are sides too. They follow the text direction, and the arrow turns
with them.

## Anatomy [#anatomy]

Base UI renders the parts and places them; OwsemUI styles the popup and the arrow, sets the 8px
offset, and carries the text direction into the portal. Provider, Root, and Portal have no box of
their own. Inspect (I) outlines the other parts on the stage; pick a name, here or on the stage, to
see its tokens.

| Part | Element | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider | None | The group: one `delay`, `closeDelay`, and `timeout` for the tooltips inside |
| Root | None | One tooltip: `open`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, and `disabled` |
| <Part name="Trigger" /> | Your button | Passed to `render`; `data-popup-open` while its tooltip shows |
| Portal | `div` | Moves the tooltip to `body`, or into `container` |
| Positioner | `div.ow-tooltip-positioner` | `side`, `align`, `sideOffset`, and `dir` |
| <Part name="Popup" /> | `div.ow-tooltip-popup` | `data-side`, and `data-instant` when it skips the animation |
| <Part name="Arrow" /> | `div.ow-tooltip-arrow` | Optional and `aria-hidden`; a triangle in a 12px box |
| <Part name="Shortcut" /> | `kbd` | Optional, in the popup after the label |

## States and variants [#states]

The stage sets the tooltip in both themes: a label, a label with its shortcut, and one without the
arrow. The surface is solid in both, with no edge and no shadow: dark on a light page, a lighter
dark on a dark one. One line is 24px tall.

| State | What shows it |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting | Nothing, for the delay |
| Entering | From 0.97 and transparent to full size, in 125 ms, eased out |
| Open | `data-side` on the popup and the arrow, `data-popup-open` on the trigger |
| At once | `data-instant`: `delay` beside an open one, `focus` from the keyboard, `dismiss` after Escape |
| Leaving | Back to 0.97 and transparent, in 100 ms |

## Keyboard and accessibility [#keyboard]

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tab | Focus on a trigger opens its tooltip at once |
| Escape | Shuts the tooltip; focus stays on the trigger |

A tooltip is a visual label only. Screen readers read the trigger's own name, so an icon button
keeps its `aria-label`, and the tooltip repeats it. Touch screens show no tooltips, since a tap also
presses the button: anything a touch user needs belongs on the page, or in a Popover.

With `prefers-reduced-motion`, tooltips come and go without any animation. In forced colors
(Windows High Contrast), the popup keeps a 1px edge in the system color, and the arrow stays solid.

## Customization [#customization]

The stage controls are open: pick the side, the arrow, the delay, or the colors, and the code
follows.

Tooltip reads these theme tokens, shared by every tooltip in the app: set them on `:root`, for each
theme. For one tooltip, set them on its Popup with a class, as `tip.css` does. A class around the
trigger does not reach, since the tooltip renders in a portal, outside the trigger's subtree.

| Theme token | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `--ow-tooltip-bg` | Surface |
| `--ow-tooltip-fg` | Text; the shortcut takes it at 80%, on a faint key |
| `--ow-radius` | Corners, 2px tighter; the shortcut's are half of it |

## Ship it [#ship]

The code drawer writes the toolbar exactly as the stage is set up. Change the side, the arrow, the
delay, or a color, and the lines that change light up.

| Part | Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Provider | `delay` | `number` | The trigger's |
| Provider | `timeout` | `number` | `400` |
| Root | `open`, `defaultOpen` | `boolean` | `false` |
| Root | `onOpenChange` | `(open, details) => void` | |
| Root | `disabled` | `boolean` | `false` |
| Trigger | `delay` | `number` | `600` |
| Trigger | `closeDelay` | `number` | `0` |
| Trigger | `closeOnClick` | `boolean` | `true` |
| Positioner | `side` | `"top" \| "right" \| "bottom" \| "left" \| "inline-start" \| "inline-end"` | `"top"` |
| Positioner | `sideOffset` | `number` | `8` |
| Positioner | `align` | `"start" \| "center" \| "end"` | `"center"` |
| Every part | `className`, `style`, `render` | | |

Everything but the 8px `sideOffset` comes from Base UI, which documents the rest, such as triggers
placed apart from their tooltip with `Tooltip.createHandle()`. Refs go to each part's element.

Import it from `owsem-ui/base`. In the registry, it is the `tooltip` item; the toolbar here also
needs `toolbar` and `toggle`.
